# Falling in Fall
A squirrel must climb a mysterious tree in the darkness. Use your flashlight to reveal the path ahead, navigate platforms, and make your way toward the top. Watch your surroundings carefully—some platforms may not be as safe as they seem!

## Controls

- **A / D:** Move left and right
- **Space:** Jump
- **F:** Toggle flashlight
